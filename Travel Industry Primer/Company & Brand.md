# Company & Brand

How [[Christopherson Business Travel]] and Andavo relate, the brand rule of thumb, the heritage stats worth memorizing, and the brand voice.

## Two names, one company

| Brand | What it is | Lead with it when... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Christopherson Business Travel** | The corporate entity and the **human-services brand** — the people, the heritage, the advisor relationships. | You talk about **who we are as a company**. |
| **Andavo** | The **technology platform brand** — the admin tools, the traveler app, the booking and payment systems we're building. | You talk about **the platform a client logs into**. |

### Brand rule of thumb

- **Heritage stats** (founded 1953, 530+ employees, 1,000+ clients, 97% retention) → attribute to **Christopherson**.
- **Product stats** (the platform, the admin app, the traveler experience) → attribute to **Andavo**.

## Brand voice — in one page

When you write a follow-up email, post in a client Slack channel, or build a deck, you are speaking for the brand. The voice is best described as **a well-dressed person explaining something simply** — smart casual in word form. Not corporate, not stiff, but not cute either.

### The five rules

1. **Lead with what the solution actually does** — wit is welcome but never at the expense of clarity.
2. **State capabilities as facts, not boasts.** No superlatives unless backed by verified data.
3. **Fewer words win** — headlines are 3 to 8 words; elevator pitches cap at 25.
4. **Specifics beat generalizations** — use numbers, names, and concrete outcomes.
5. **Even technical solutions get framed through the person they help** — human outcome first, mechanism second.
