# Deprecated Terms

Legacy [[Christopherson Business Travel]] / Andavo product names that **should not appear in any external content**. They show up in old client documentation and old emails — when you see them, mentally translate to the current term. (Internalize this list before your first independent client touchpoint; see [[Client-Facing Roles]].)

## Don't say / Say instead / Why

| Don't say | Say instead | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AirPortal** | Andavo Admin | AirPortal was the legacy admin platform brand. Fully replaced. Legacy URLs redirect. |
| **VirtualPay / VirtualPay Lite** | Andavo Payments | Virtual card capability rebranded under Andavo Payments. VirtualPay Lite is sunsetting. |
| **SecurityLogic** | Risk Management | Capability moved into Andavo Admin under the Risk Management name. |
| **AirBank** | unused ticket management | Lowercase descriptor — not a product brand anymore. |
| **Prime Analytics** | Sustainability Reporting | Renamed in 2026 to better reflect actual scope (carbon emissions, benchmarking). |
| **Value Summary / Value Scorecard** | program performance reporting / Travel Data & Analytics │ Domo | Legacy report product names. Use descriptive language now. |
| **Conferma** | Andavo Payments (or just "the platform") | Conferma is invisible infrastructure. Do not name in external content. |
| **Concur Elite Partner** | SAP Concur Partner [[Glossary#TMC]] | The Elite/Select tier structure was discontinued in **October 2025**. |
