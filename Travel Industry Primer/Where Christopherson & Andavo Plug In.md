# Where Christopherson & Andavo Plug In

The [[Travel Industry Primer/Travel Industry Primer|field guide]] is intentionally vendor-agnostic about the ecosystem at large. But you work for [[Christopherson Business Travel|Christopherson]] and sell Andavo, so this maps our footprint onto the fifteen [[Ecosystem Layers]]. Use it when a customer asks *"what do you actually do, vs. what do you partner for?"*

**Role key:** **Native** = we replace that vendor · **Partner** = we plug into it · **Partner referral** = we refer the customer to it.

| Layer | Role | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| 1. Profile and identity | **Native** | Andavo manages traveler profiles natively; SSO supported via standard identity providers. |
| 2. Online booking tool | **Native** | Andavo Online is our [[Glossary#OBT|OBT]]. We can also operate behind Concur Travel, Deem, and others when a customer requires. |
| 3. TMC | **Native** | This is who we are. |
| 4. GDS and NDC | **Agnostic** | Multi-GDS, [[Glossary#NDC|NDC]] channels, direct connects. Best-fare logic across sources is a deliberate differentiator. |
| 5. Hotel sourcing | **Native + partner** | Internal sourcing capability, supplemented by BCD partnership for the broader 49,700-property network. |
| 6. Ground transport | **Native + partner** | GDS-based car rental, integrations with major rideshare and chauffeur platforms. |
| 7. Cards and payment | **Native + partner** | Standard integrations with major card issuers, Conferma virtual cards, and central pay configurations. |
| 8. Expense | **Partner** | SAP Concur Partner TMC. Tight integrations with Concur Expense; data flows into other major expense systems on customer request. |
| 9. Reporting | **Native + partner** | Andavo platform reporting plus DataFlex Net integrations for multi-TMC consolidations and customer BI feeds. |
| 10. Risk and duty of care | **Native + partner** | Itinerary feeds to all major duty-of-care providers (ISOS, Crisis24, etc.) on customer request. |
| 11. Sustainability | **Partner** | Emissions data through reporting and partner platforms. |
| 12. Visa | **Partner referral** | Standard referral relationships with major visa vendors. |
| 13. Insurance | **Partner referral** | Typically arranged outside the TMC; TMC can flag and refer. |
| 14. Quality control | **Native** | Mid-office quality control native; rate auditing and fare optimization tools deployed program-by-program. |
| 15. Meetings and events | **Native** | Christopherson Meetings & Events division for group air, hotel blocks, and event coordination. |

## Why this matters in conversations

> When a customer tells you they are using Vendor X for a particular layer, you can immediately tell them whether **we replace** that vendor (native role), **we plug into it** (partner role), or **we refer** them to it (partner referral). You will sound like you actually know the ecosystem — because by the time you finish reading the guide, you will.
