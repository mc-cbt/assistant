# Service Delivery Models

Every account at [[Christopherson Business Travel]] / Andavo fits one of **three service delivery models**. The model dictates how the program runs, which solutions to lead with, and what fee structure applies. As a [[Client-Facing Roles|CSM]] working small accounts, you'll see all three — but the **dominant pattern in your book will be Managed Self-Serve**.

## The three models

| Model | What it looks like | Lead solutions | Fee model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Managed Self-Serve** *(your dominant model)* | [[Glossary#OBT]]-primary. Travelers book independently. Advisors are an **exception channel** for complex itineraries and emergencies, not the default path. | Concur Travel or Deem (OBT), Andavo Admin (program control), Risk Management. | Lower per-transaction fees on online; higher on offline/agent. |
| **Managed Full-Service** | The default [[Glossary#TMC]] model — we manage end-to-end. Advisors handle complex itineraries and exceptions. | Advisor Services (lead with experience, 24/7 coverage), OBT secondary. | Standard per-transaction fee model. |
| **Onsite / Hosted** | Embedded advisor model. We deploy advisors as the client's internal travel team. **A different sales motion entirely.** | Trip Request & Proposal (intake), Advisor Tools (GDS depth). | Custom commercial model — **not** per-transaction. |

## Important nuances

- **Self-Serve:** Don't lead with advisor services — it undercuts the value prop.
- **Full-Service:** Don't position consulting as included — it's an **optional add-on**.
- **Onsite:** Don't describe after-hours as dedicated onsite — it's **DNIS transfer to the standard team**.
