# Vendor Cheat Sheet

Quick reference. When a customer drops a vendor name, glance here. Names you should be able to place at a glance, organized by layer. See [[Ecosystem Layers]] for the full detail and [[Where Christopherson & Andavo Plug In]] for our footprint.

## TMCs

| Vendor | Camp | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| AmexGBT | Service-heavy legacy | Largest globally. Acquired CWT and Egencia. Heavy enterprise. |
| BCD Travel | Service-heavy legacy | Privately held. Strong global, especially EMEA. |
| FCM | Service-heavy legacy | Owned by Flight Centre. Known for enterprise mid-market depth. |
| CWT | Legacy (now AmexGBT) | Acquired by AmexGBT. Watch for migration churn. |
| Direct ATPI | Tech-forward | Maritime, energy, marine specialty. Growing in mainstream corporate. |
| [[Christopherson Business Travel|Christopherson]] / Andavo | Tech-forward | Where you work. |
| Navan | Tech-first | All-in-one travel + expense + cards. Strong UX, weaker service depth. |
| Egencia | Tech-first (now AmexGBT) | Was Expedia, then Amex GBT. Mid-market roots; transition ongoing. |
| TravelBank, Perk | Tech-first niche | Smaller players in the modern UX category. |

## OBTs

| OBT | Owner | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Concur Travel | SAP Concur | Dominant enterprise [[Glossary#OBT|OBT]]. TMC-agnostic. Modernization in progress. |
| Cytric | Amadeus | Enterprise OBT, EU strength, Microsoft Teams integration. |
| Spotnana | Spotnana | Modern, API-first. Sold as OBT or full TMC backbone. |
| Deem | Étraveli | Modern OBT used by mid-market TMCs as Concur alternative. |
| Navan | Navan | Native to Navan TMC, cannot be used standalone. |
| Egencia OBT | Amex GBT | Native to Egencia / Amex GBT mid-market. |
| Andavo Online | Christopherson | Our OBT. |

## Expense systems

| System | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| SAP Concur Expense | The market giant. Default in enterprise. |
| Navan Expense | Bundled with Navan; not standalone. |
| Expensify | SMB and mid-market. Mobile-first. |
| Ramp | Cards-first platform with expense built in. Aggressive mid-market growth. |
| Brex | Cards + expense for venture-backed companies. |
| Emburse (Certify, Chrome River, Captio, Tallie) | Mid-market and enterprise alternative to Concur. |
| Workday Expenses | For Workday HR and Finance customers. |
