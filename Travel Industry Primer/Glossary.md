# Glossary

Consolidated glossary of corporate-travel and TMC acronyms and terms, ordered alphabetically. Link to any entry as `[[Glossary#TERM]]`.

### ARC
Airlines Reporting Corporation. Manages ticket settlement between airlines and agencies in the U.S.

### Attachment rate
Percent of trips where a hotel (or car) is booked through the program. A key program-health metric, especially for hotel programs.

### Back-office
The TMC's accounting and finance system.

### Best fare
The lowest applicable fare given policy, routing, and timing rules. Often calculated by TMC algorithms.

### BSP
Billing and Settlement Plan. The IATA equivalent of ARC, used outside the U.S.

### BTA
Business Travel Account. Amex-specific term for a central pay / lodge account.

### Concur
SAP Concur. Dominant expense-management platform; also a major OBT (Concur Travel).

### CSRD
Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive. EU regulation driving sustainability reporting requirements.

### Direct connect
API integration directly with a supplier (especially airlines), bypassing the GDS.

### DEFRA
UK Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs. A common emissions-calculation methodology.

### DNIS transfer
Dialed Number Identification Service routing. Used for after-hours coverage on onsite/hosted programs.

### Duty of care
The legal and ethical responsibility an employer has to keep traveling employees safe. Andavo's Risk Management is its duty-of-care product.

### Enterprise
Generally $50M+ in annual travel spend; global programs.

### ESG
Environmental, Social, Governance. The broader frame inside which sustainability reporting sits.

### Folio capture
Pulling hotel folio data from the property after checkout for reconciliation. Andavo handles this in-house now (used to be Conferma).

### FOP
Form of Payment. The card or method used at point of booking. See also the [[Projects/FOP/FOP|FOP]] project.

### GDS
Global Distribution System. The wholesale travel inventory pipe / data backbone connecting airlines, hotels, and cars to agencies. Sabre, Travelport (Galileo/Worldspan), Amadeus. Andavo is GDS-agnostic — most legacy TMCs are not.

### GHG Protocol
Greenhouse Gas Protocol. The most widely used emissions-accounting standard.

### IATA
International Air Transport Association. Industry body that sets airline distribution standards (including NDC).

### KTN
Known Traveler Number. The TSA PreCheck identifier.

### Leakage
Bookings made outside the program. Industry-typical 10–25%.

### Lodge card / central pay
A single card on file at the TMC that all bookings charge to.

### Loyalty / FF#
Frequent flyer number; loyalty number for airlines, hotels, or car companies. Stored in the traveler profile.

### Mid-market
Roughly $5M to $50M in annual travel spend; sometimes defined by employee count (100–5,000).

### Mid-office
The TMC's internal system that processes bookings between the OBT/agent and the GDS/supplier.

### Mid-office QC
Quality-control scripts running in the TMC's mid-office to catch errors and policy issues.

### NDC
New Distribution Capability. The newer, airline-direct content channel that bypasses the GDS; an IATA standard for airlines to push richer content directly to agencies. Increasingly important for fare access.

### OBT
Online Booking Tool. The traveler-facing self-service booking interface where travelers book their own trips. The main OBTs are Concur Travel and Deem (and eventually Andavo Booking).

### Online adoption rate
Percentage of bookings made through the OBT vs. through an advisor. Higher = more cost-efficient program. A common KPI and key program-health metric.

### Out-of-policy
A booking that violates the company's travel policy. Either flagged, blocked, or required to have a justification.

### P&L
Profit and loss. CSMs drive profit through efficient configurations and technology adoption.

### PNR
Passenger Name Record. The reservation record in the GDS — a collection of air, car, or hotel reservations holding passenger names, segments, payment, and special requests. Each booking has one.

### Pre-trip approval
Workflow that pauses a booking for manager or finance approval before ticketing.

### QBR / EBR
Quarterly Business Review / Executive Business Review. The traditional cadence call with a client. Andavo is moving away from default QBRs for small accounts.

### RFP
Request for Proposal. Used both for hotel sourcing (annual) and TMC selection (every 2–3 years).

### Savings
Difference between a published fare and the negotiated fare actually paid. A core reporting metric.

### SBT
Self-Booking Tool. Older term for OBT; still appears in legacy documents.

### SMB
Small and medium business; under $5M annual travel spend.

### SMM
Strategic Meetings Management. Treating meetings and events as a managed category.

### TMC
Travel Management Company. The contracted vendor that manages a corporate travel program. That's us (Andavo / Christopherson). Also Navan, BCD, AmexGBT, FCM, Direct ATPI.

### Unused ticket
A ticket whose value can be reused on a future booking. Tracked by the TMC; managing them well saves clients real money.

### Virtual card (VCC)
A single-use credit card number generated for one booking. Reduces fraud risk and improves reconciliation. Core to Andavo Payments. Other providers: Conferma, AirPlus, eNett, WEX.
