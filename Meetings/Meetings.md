# Meetings

Hub for meeting notes. One note per meeting, named `<Topic> Meeting YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Purpose

Write-ups of meetings worth remembering — summary, decisions, and action items —
usually generated from the Teams transcript via the `/meeting-summary` command
(`calendar-lookup` → `meeting-transcript` → `meeting-note`).

Not every meeting needs a note. Standups generally don't; decisions do.
