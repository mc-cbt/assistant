## Discussion

### GBTA 2026 recap

- GBTA is the company's **largest event of the year** on the corporate travel side — every
  major airline, hotelier, supplier, competing TMC, and **500–750 travel managers/buyers**.
- Company booked **~$725M in travel** last year across the whole business.
- Pipeline into the show: **>$400M** in prospects had RSVP'd to the dinner, booked booth
  demos, or committed to stop by. By the end of the show, **>$1B in prospects** had come
  through the booth.
- **135 badge scans** — roughly flat vs. prior years — but only **8 were vendors/suppliers**,
  so the **buyer percentage was unusually high** and **average deal size went way up**.
- Named opportunities moving through the funnel (~**$85M already in later stages**):
  - **Oshkosh** — ~$25M; **invited into the final RFP that morning**, one of three, in a top
    position.
  - **Sentara Health** _(transcript reads "Centara" — Sentara is the likely reading)_ — ~$20M.
  - **Vizient** _(transcript reads "Visient")_ — ~$18M; team flying down in **November**.
  - **SEIU** — ~$11M.
  - **St. Jude Research** — $5–10M; visiting the office **next month**.
  - **Midmark**, **Affinity**, **MacArthur Foundation** — ~$2–4M each.
- Booth meetings with **top-50 enterprise programs**: hour-long demos for **Google** and
  **PricewaterhouseCoopers**; a short meeting with **Apple**; plus the **Department of War**,
  **United States Postal Service**, and **Yum Brands** (Taco Bell, KFC).
- **Strategic read:** competitors are either **tech-first** or **human/professional-services-first**;
  the market is not responding to that either/or. Christopherson/Andavo's positioning as a
  **continuum** — high-touch service *and* modern in-house technology — is what buyers
  responded to. Josh called this out explicitly as validation for the **product and
  engineering org**.
- Caveat stated plainly: **how much converts is unknown**; sales is working the contact list
  hard and this is the largest conversion opportunity in company history.

### Team changes

- A **few org changes** prompted by the show — specifically making sure **service and client
  servicing** are ready if this business onboards.
- Described as **small iterations**, not a restructure; **most people on the call unaffected**.
- To be **announced the following morning (2026-08-19)**.

### TinyPulse rollout

- New tool for **anonymous employee feedback**, live as of that morning.
- Two entry points: the **TinyPulse bot in Teams** (surveys, suggestions, all in-app) and
  **tinypulse.com** (SSO — no separate login).
- Admin view shows **no name, department, or identifying metadata**; admins can reply
  privately and the exchange stays anonymous on the employee's side. Employees see who is
  responding.
- Also carries a **"wins" board** so responses to suggestions are visible.
- **First survey going out the next day** — longer than usual because it's the first; after
  that, short **weekly touchpoints**.
- Emails from the tool state explicitly whether a given response is anonymous.
