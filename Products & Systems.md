# Products & Systems

A glossary of the products, platforms, and external systems referenced across these notes. See [[People/People|People]] for people and [[People/Org Chart|Org Chart]] for reporting structure.

## Our products

### Andavo (software product)
The company's modern travel management software product, and the name the company ([[Christopherson Business Travel]]) is transitioning its identity toward. Customers currently on **Air Portal** are planned to migrate to Andavo starting **2026-09-30**.

### Air Portal
The **legacy** travel management software, used by **most current customers**. There is a plan to transition customers off Air Portal and onto **Andavo** beginning **2026-09-30**. Maintained by the [[Velocity]] team.

### Red App
A small application that lives **inside the Sabre webpage**, built with a **limited subset of JavaScript and React components**. It lets us expose **Andavo-specific UI and functionality directly inside Sabre**, where travel agents work. Built and maintained by the [[Velocity]] team.

## External systems

### Sabre
A **GDS** (see below) that the company integrates with. Hosts our [[#Red App]] inside its webpage.

### GDS (Global Distribution System)
A pipeline for essentially **all travel inventory**. A GDS lets agents book hotels, air, rental cars, etc. across essentially all airlines, hotel chains, and rental providers from one place. **Sabre** is the GDS we work with.
