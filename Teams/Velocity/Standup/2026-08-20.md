## Discussion — IC service fee override and QC-only checkbox

- **IC booking service fee override**: settled as a **new override, "invoice service fee
  only"**, plus a **remark for fee only** so Complete knows it isn't ticketing. If the fee
  differs from what Complete finds in its table, the advisor adds the **additional service
  fee override**. Tanner is clear to proceed.
- **QA/QC-only checkbox for ICs** is technically an override too, but it lives with
  **queuing to Complete** and is meant to be **IC-only** — which is why it's gated on
  being able to **control a feature by user or client**.
- Two candidate mechanisms: a **PostHog feature flag** (discussed, not built; assigned to
  [[Jake Knowlton|Jake]], no work done yet) or a **client setting/configuration**.
- Concern raised against client configurations: they'd **show up for every client** and
  clutter Andavo, especially for exception cases that only apply to ICs.

## Discussion — internal-only client settings

- **[[Matt Condie|Matt]]** asked whether we have the concept of a **client configuration
  visible only in the internal app** — something we can turn on for a client without them
  seeing or controlling it. His broader idea: **capability flags** (not feature flags,
  which shouldn't be permanent) that we toggle per client internally.
- **[[Chad Maughan|Chad]]**: we **already have internal client settings** built out and
  they aren't returned to the client. Prefers to **keep using client settings** rather
  than introduce a new "client configuration" concept.
- Chad's caution: don't **codify on-site vs. regular client** distinctions into the
  system. A client's advisor situation changes (his example: a client that had advisors,
  dropped them, is hiring them back) and we shouldn't have to re-implement the account.
  An **abstraction on top of client settings** — a client profile that implies a typical
  set of settings — is fine as long as it's **escapable**.

## Discussion — Complete, QC, and the direction of travel

- Chad on ICs **ticketing themselves**: **no**. "That's exactly how we get debit memos,"
  and it runs opposite to the direction of **workflows**.
- Chad on the **documentation requirement**: agrees it's heavy-handed. The data collected
  is low quality (advisors padding text to clear the five-word minimum, or dropping into
  the command line to bypass it). We already have **trip diffs** and can **audit advisor
  commands**, so the audit trail should be automatic rather than typed.
- Chad on the **async queue-to-Complete QC pattern**: wants it gone. QC should be
  **synchronous and live with the advisor**, with real-time feedback, rather than queued
  into a black box. A **Complete API call doesn't require** the async pattern.
- Idea floated: encode **QC checks as workflows** — the same machinery as **Approvals** —
  so QC rules can be **configured** rather than hand-written in VB script, eventually
  self-serve for clients within guardrails. Framed as necessary to scale: **~88 people**
  currently work in and around Complete.
- Chad's stated preference: spend time **replacing Complete** rather than building IC-only
  features.

## Discussion — why the IC rollout stalled

- Context Chad gave: at **Andavo Connections in Nashville (May)** the leisure/IC advisor
  board asked for a full tech demo. ICs saw **Sabre Red Web** point-and-click booking and
  were ready to be implemented — many had been telling their travelers to book their own
  air because they believed the GDS was command-line only.
- After that, **arbitrary blockers landed in the critical path** internally — getting rid
  of **ClientBase**, manually migrating profiles into Sabre, scraping **Apollo** to move
  profiles — even though advisors had said they'd retype profiles by hand to get access.
- Net result: only about **three ICs** ended up set up, one raised concerns, and the
  initiative stalled. Chad was asked about the lack of progress at **Virtuoso week** last
  week.
- Parallel example cited: clients "can't leave **Air Portal**" because of the
  **traveler-messaging** feature — which exists in Air Portal and has been used **once in
  three years**.
- Mitch's read from the technical side: **no red-app blockers** to ICs going live today.
  Most ICs are already **set up as agents in Andavo**; what's left is **setting them up in
  Sabre** and getting the write-up assigned.
