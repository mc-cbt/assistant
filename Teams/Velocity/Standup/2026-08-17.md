## Discussion — service fee override and Concur Complete

- **The service fee override remark is formatted wrong** and is breaking **Complete**. The
  **amount needs to come before** the per-ticket / per-PNR / per-pax token — i.e. the
  amount and the "per" segment need to **swap**, with the date last. Complete receives the
  remark but can't evaluate it, so it doesn't know what to charge.
  - Tanner: **a quick configuration change**; he'll do it.
- **Underlying structural problem.** Complete's default path is to **modify an existing
  remark line**, so it expects a **line number**. Because the override lives in **Andavo**
  and never gets written to the PNR, there is no line number — Complete writes it as
  **line 0** and **fails 100% of the time**, so the ticketing routine never picks the
  remark up.
  - Current state: the Complete-side owner has a **manual workaround** (adding the remark
    by hand so a line number exists), so this isn't urgent — but it's a risk for **every
    other override**, not just service fees.
  - Constraint: **[[Joshua Cameron|Josh]] does not want this documented into the PNR**,
    which is what makes "just write it to the PNR" unattractive.
  - Two options raised: **write the override into the PNR as well as Andavo**, or **have
    Complete handle a missing line number**. Stated preference was to **solve it on the
    Complete side rather than bake a workaround into Andavo**.
  - Historical context: few people use the fee override; agents generally take what Air
    Portal gives them, and when the fee doesn't charge they **add the remark to the PNR by
    hand** and re-send to Complete.
