## Post-standup — CHG duplicate-profile investigation

Extended debugging session between [[Kyle Casazza]] and [[Mitchell Cannon|Mitch]] after the
standup ended. Individual traveler details are deliberately omitted here — see the ticket
and the systems of record.

- **Symptom:** one CHG traveler has **two profiles** — an older one created via the **HR
  feed** and a newer one created in **Andavo** — sharing the same email and tied to the
  same Air Portal user. A trip is associated to the wrong one. **Deem** and **Sabre** both
  show related errors.
- **Working hypothesis (two compounding problems):**
  1. The traveler's **email changed** on the HR feed (Oct 2025), but Andavo **silently
     refused to apply the email update** — the resolver matches on **Faces UUID** first
     and the code path blocks email changes. So Andavo kept the stale address.
  2. Later (Jan 2026), a **trip import** arrived with the *new* email, matched nothing,
     and — during the window when Andavo was still **auto-creating profiles from trip
     imports** — **created a second user**. That behavior was turned off early 2026 after
     complaints on an expense client.
- **Constraints hit while investigating:** no **audit history** in Andavo before ~Jan
  2026; HR-feed processed-file backups only go back **30 days**; Deem has poor change
  history; the utilities table is too slow to load.
- **HR feed mechanics recapped** (for the record): the feed diffs the new file against the
  stored previous file by **employee ID**, applies adds/removes/field changes, pushes to
  **Air Portal** and to **Faces**, and Faces fans out to **Concur / Deem / Sabre**. Open
  question raised: whether **Faces → Andavo** propagation was correctly wired at the time
  of the October change, and whether HR-feed changes have been tested end-to-end against
  Andavo.
- Found an **existing open Jira ticket (10259)** covering email changes, created by
  [[Kyle Crowther]] with a Lucid flow (Andavo → Auth0 email process) but little written
  detail. Decided to reuse it rather than open a new feature ticket.
