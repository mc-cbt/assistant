## Discussion — Crisis 24 and the risk map

- **Closing the side panel leaves the map filtered** to the selected alert, so travellers
  disappear from view with no obvious cause. Came up repeatedly in demos, so customers will
  hit it. There *is* a **filter chip** at the top, but it isn't clear enough — proposal to
  **highlight it** (e.g. blue with a count) so the applied filter is obvious. Both
  behaviours have a valid use case (closing for screen real estate vs. clearing the filter),
  so the fix is the **indication**, not necessarily auto-clearing.
- **Deselecting an alert should zoom back out** to the previous/default map state.
- **Date-range mismatch**: the alert's date range isn't the map's. An alert spanning three
  months reports ~30 travellers affected while the map shows five. Ticket exists somewhere.
- **"Limit alerts to impacted trips"** looked broken in demos but turned out to just be
  **slow** — needs a **better loading indicator**.
- The **"see details" page** can be **killed** in favour of a modal now that the map has
  **polygons**; two or three existing map tickets cover this.
